# Service Level Agreements (SLA)

## What is an SLA?
A Service Level Agreement (SLA) is the maximum time within which the municipal corporation promises to resolve a specific type of complaint.

## Category SLA Timelines

### Garbage & Sanitation
- **Street Sweeping:** 24 Hours
- **Garbage Collection (Missed pickup):** 24 Hours
- **Illegal Dump Yard Clearing:** 48 Hours
- **Dead Animal Removal:** 12 Hours

### Roads & Infrastructure
- **Pothole Repair:** 72 Hours (3 Days)
- **Broken Footpath:** 7 Days
- **Streetlight Not Working:** 48 Hours
- **Fallen Tree / Branches:** 24 Hours

### Drainage & Water
- **Sewage Overflow:** 24 Hours
- **Missing Manhole Cover:** 12 Hours (High Priority)
- **No Water Supply:** 24 Hours
- **Contaminated Water:** 24 Hours

## What happens if the SLA is breached?
If a complaint is not resolved within the SLA deadline, it is automatically escalated to the next supervisory level (e.g., from Field Officer to Zonal Officer). If it remains unresolved, it is further escalated to the Department Head.
